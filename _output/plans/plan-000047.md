## Brief

> agora que salvamos os embeddings na criacao dos posts, nao precisamos recalcula-los na geracao dos clusters, podemos recuperar a informaçao já salva via api.

## Context

When a post is created (via `POST /citizen_posts/` or `POST /citizen_posts/bulk`), `embed_and_store()` is called immediately, persisting the embedding to ChromaDB keyed by `post.id`.

`build_cluster_df()` in `fala-gavea/src/fala_gavea/pipeline/cluster.py` currently calls:
1. `embed_and_store(posts, vectorstore_dir)` — re-encodes all posts unconditionally
2. `get_embeddings(ids, vectorstore_dir)` — retrieves from ChromaDB

The re-encoding in step 1 is now redundant. Embeddings are always persisted at post-creation time — this is an invariant of the system. `build_cluster_df` can therefore call `get_embeddings` directly and remove `embed_and_store` entirely.
